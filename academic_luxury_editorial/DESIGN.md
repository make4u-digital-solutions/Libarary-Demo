## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, academic-luxury aesthetic designed for premium physical study libraries, quiet working sanctuaries, and executive reading lounges. It evokes serenity, scholarly focus, intellectual ambition, and timeless hospitality. The visual mood departs from stark, sterile co-working environments by drawing upon historic university libraries, classic reading halls, and bespoke private member clubs.

The design movement blends **warm editorial minimalism** with **tactile luxury accents**:
- Deep forest greens and warm ivory surfaces form a calm, low-glare foundation ideal for deep concentration.
- Subtle champagne-gold trims, delicate hairline borders, and warm ambient backdrops invoke prestige and thoughtful craftsmanship.
- Typography celebrates the pairing of high-contrast editorial serifs with crisp, hyper-legible modern grotesks.
- Visual elements feature smooth radius treatments, pill-shaped tags, and structured horizontal cards with photography that conveys tangible acoustic privacy and warmth.

## Colors

The palette balances deep organic shadows with bright, inviting parchment tones:

- **Primary (`#10271E` - Deep Forest Green):** Anchors high-priority calls to action, rich dark hero sections, footer backgrounds, selected states, and primary brand insignia. Conveys deep intellectual quietude and prestige.
- **Secondary (`#D9AD62` - Champagne Gold):** Serves as an accent and luxury signifier. Used for badge fills, interactive focus outlines, golden icons, featured card badges (e.g., "Popular"), and editorial italic emphasis.
- **Tertiary (`#EDE5D8` - Soft Beige / Cream Border):** Acts as the standard container stroke, pill-button border, card partition, and muted card background in neutral compositions.
- **Neutral Surface (`#F8F4EC` - Warm Ivory):** The main canvas background for daytime reading zones. It eliminates the eye strain of harsh blue-white screens and reproduces the warmth of premium heavy-stock book paper.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for elevated card containers, input field backgrounds, and modal cards resting over the warm ivory canvas.
- **Charcoal Black (`#171916`):** The primary typographic ink color for headings and body content on light surfaces, ensuring AAA contrast while remaining softer than pure `#000000`.

## Typography

The typographic system pairs the literary authority of **Playfair Display** with the clear geometric utility of **DM Sans**:

- **Playfair Display (Headings & Display):** Evokes classic scholarly tomes, literary magazines, and dignified academic institutions. Use regular and semi-bold weights for display titles, section headings, and promotional lockups. Italicized cuts in headings are deliberately leveraged to bring warmth, human touch, and refined cadence to key phrases (e.g., *Serious Learners*).
- **DM Sans (Body, Microcopy, and UI Elements):** High x-height, neutral geometry, and open apertures provide effortless legibility across dense schedules, amenity grids, seat selection tables, and pricing figures.
- **Rhythm & Line Heights:** Headings maintain a tight 1.15 to 1.25 line-height ratio to preserve editorial impact. Body copy uses a relaxed 1.5 to 1.6 ratio to replicate comfortable editorial reading experiences.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a structured 12-column grid with deliberate breathing room to mirror the quiet, uncluttered physical spaces it represents:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max-width container of 1280px, centered with 48px (`3rem`) page margins and 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters. Content sections are bounded and generous, using `space-xl` (40px) or `space-xl * 2` (80px) for vertical section gaps.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 32px margins and 20px gutters. Three-column pricing and feature modules collapse into 2-column or stacked grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) margins and 16px gutters. Horizontal card layouts (image + specifications + CTA) reflow cleanly into vertical stacked cards.
- **Component Padding Scale:** Internal card padding defaults to `space-lg` (24px). Micro-components like badges, chip filters, and buttons leverage `space-xs` (4px) to `space-md` (16px) for balanced, proportional tap targets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through subtle tonal layering and soft ambient lighting rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base layer: Warm ivory (`#F8F4EC`) or deep forest canvas (`#10271E`).
  - Mid layer (Cards & Panels): Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or deep tinted forest surface (`#163227`).
  - High layer (Modals, Overlays, Dropdowns): Pure white elevated with soft diffusion.
- **Ambient Diffusion:** Shadows are tinted with forest-charcoal tones (`rgba(16, 39, 30, 0.06)`) using an extended blur radius (20px to 32px) and minimal Y-offset (4px to 8px), evoking the gentle, soft-cast illumination of library reading lamps.
- **Hairline Framing:** Card perimeters and dividers employ low-contrast 1px strokes in `#EDE5D8` (on ivory) or translucent champagne borders `rgba(217, 173, 98, 0.25)` (on deep forest). This gives components a crisp, bookbinder-grade structure.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Rounded** geometry (Base 0.5rem / 8px) with bespoke extensions for pills and large container envelopes:

- **Base Radius (8px / `0.5rem`):** Applied to form fields, nested images within cards, amenity icons, and micro-steppers.
- **Large Radius (16px / `1rem`):** Standard for main content cards, horizontal study space cards, pricing tiers, and photo gallery cells.
- **Extra-Large Radius (24px / `1.5rem`):** Used for primary section containers, dark hero banners, and promotional wrappers.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Used exclusively for contextual tag badges (e.g., "Our Study Spaces", "Membership Plans"), duration segment switchers (Daily / Monthly / Quarterly), filter pills, and primary CTAs. This lends friendly ergonomics and tactile distinction.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Deep forest green background (`#10271E`), pure white text, pill-shaped radius (`9999px`), 14px DM Sans SemiBold, 12px vertical and 24px horizontal padding. Features a right-aligned hairline arrow icon (`->`). On hover: subtle scale transform (1.02) and champagne glow border.
- **Secondary / Accent Button:** Champagne gold fill (`#D9AD62`), charcoal text (`#171916`), pill-shaped. Reserved for high-value immediate reservations.
- **Outline Button:** Transparent background, 1px stroke in `#EDE5D8` or `#10271E`, 14px DM Sans SemiBold, charcoal text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Section Pill:** Small centered pill placed above major headings. Translucent champagne background (`rgba(217, 173, 98, 0.15)`), thin gold border (`#D9AD62`), gold micro-icon, and 11px uppercase/semi-bold DM Sans text.
- **Filter Chips:** Pill shape with neutral ivory background and hairline border (`#EDE5D8`). Active state flips to deep forest green (`#10271E`) with white text.

### Cards
- **Space Showcase Horizontal Card:** Dual-pane arrangement. Left side houses a 16:10 rounded architectural photograph. Right side features space title, capacity/vibe description, bulleted amenity checklist with check icons, and a right-aligned pricing + "Book Now" CTA pill.
- **Membership Plan Card:** Vertical white card with 16px radius and 1px border (`#EDE5D8`). Highlighted plans (e.g., "Premium") feature a champagne gold crown tag ("Popular") at top-right, accompanied by dark green fill for the primary CTA button.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Date & Time Pickers:** Clean white surfaces with 8px radius, 1px `#EDE5D8` border, calendar/clock lead icons, and dark charcoal text.
- **Plan Radio Selectors:** Interactive stacked cards with rounded corners. Selected state displays a gold or forest-tinted radio mark, a light cream background highlight, and clear right-aligned price bolding.

### Stepper Navigation
- Used in checkout and reservation flows. Numbered round badges connected by delicate horizontal guide lines (`1 Select Plan -> 2 Your Details -> 3 Payment -> 4 Confirmation`). Active step is highlighted in solid deep forest green.
